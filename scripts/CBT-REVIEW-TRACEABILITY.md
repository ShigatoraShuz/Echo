Implementation decisions: ordinary replies offer one concrete action when useful; questions remain optional; requested lists override brevity targets and contain exactly one to ten items. Review credentials and identifying details are excluded from prompts and fixtures.

Sentence and list completion are checked every token so they cannot skip a completed ending. Reflective questions are assessed separately from structural completeness; two questions do not make every subsequent sentence unfinished. Above ten, a deterministic limit notice corrects only a count label in the introduction; generated list items and safety qualifications are retained. Single-item lists receive their own line. Ordinary paragraph formatting allows longer coherent paragraphs while preserving every word and punctuation mark.

Read-aloud resolves the authenticated user's selected stored Buddy message by conversation/message ID, defaults to MOSS v1.5, buffers the whole reply, and cancels on message switching, navigation, or starting live Talk.
