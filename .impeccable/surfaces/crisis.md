# Crisis support surface brief

## THESIS

The page behaves like a calm emergency response sheet: one immediate action, one manageable next step, and one verified path to more help.

## OWN-WORLD

Use ECHO's fixed light botanical system: warm cream ground, forest structure, sage support areas, Cormorant emotional headings, and Manrope operational copy. Coral belongs only to the 911 action.

## STORY

1. Call now when danger is immediate.
2. Choose one safe action rather than completing a long task.
3. Use a fully user-paced breathing cue while waiting.
4. Reach verified Philippines support resources with loading, error, empty, and success states.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At 390 by 844 pixels, the 911 and NCMH call actions are fully visible without scrolling or waiting for an intro. At desktop widths, the calm reassurance and emergency actions share one split composition.

## FORM

Seed: a direct emergency response sheet translated into ECHO's editorial botanical language. The structure uses flat sections, dividing rules, and list rows instead of repeated cards. Build path: code-led within the established ECHO visual system.

## QUALITY BAR

- Emergency actions render immediately with native scrolling and no route, reveal, autoplay, or decorative motion.
- Phone links retain correct `tel:` values and self-contained accessible names.
- Body copy meets WCAG AA contrast on cream, forest, sage, and coral surfaces.
- The checklist, breathing control, and resource loading, error, empty, and success states remain keyboard accessible.
- The page has no horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1440 pixel widths.
- The crisis route remains usable without authentication and bypasses the first-visit ECHO intro.
