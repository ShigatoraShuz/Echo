# ECHO product context

ECHO is a private journaling and reflective wellbeing support product. It helps people record how they feel, notice patterns, use grounding tools, and find human support. ECHO is not a diagnostic tool, emergency monitoring service, or replacement for professional care.

The public experience should feel calm, private, botanical, and trustworthy. Cream, forest green, sage, Cormorant display type, and Manrope interface type are established brand commitments. Urgent support may use coral for the primary emergency action.

For crisis support, success means that a distressed visitor can immediately see how to call emergency services, choose one manageable safety step, and reach verified support resources without animation or sign-in friction.
